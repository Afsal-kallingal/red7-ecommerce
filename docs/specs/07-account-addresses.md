# Feature Spec: Account & Saved Addresses

## Purpose
Enables customers to view their profile, past orders, saved shipping destinations, wishlisted drops, and contact support.

## UI Behavior
- Profile card with avatar, name, email, phone, membership badge.
- Navigation links:
  - Orders & Tracking
  - Saved Delivery Addresses (Add/Edit/Delete)
  - Wishlist
  - Coupons & Rewards
  - Help Center & WhatsApp Concierge
- Address Management:
  - Address cards with default tag.
  - Modal form for Street, City, State, 6-digit PIN, Phone.
