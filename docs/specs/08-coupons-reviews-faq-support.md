# Feature Spec: Coupons, Reviews, FAQ & Direct Support

## Purpose
Supports conversion and customer assurance through discoverable promotional codes, community reviews, clear FAQs, and direct WhatsApp concierge access.

## UI Behavior
- **Coupons Page (`/coupons`)**:
  - Grid of available voucher cards (`BUY4GET1`, `MADRIDISTA`, `FIRSTDROP10`).
  - 1-tap `COPY CODE` button with toast feedback.
  - Expiry date, terms, and minimum cart value requirements.
- **Reviews Page (`/reviews`)**:
  - Average rating (`4.9 / 5.0`), total rating count, breakdown bars.
  - Review filters (Verified Buyers, With Photos, Most Recent).
  - Submit Review modal.
- **FAQ Page (`/faq`)**:
  - Categorized accordions (Shipping, Authenticity, Sizing, Returns, Payments).
- **Contact & WhatsApp Concierge (`/contact`)**:
  - Live customer support channels, WhatsApp direct launcher with pre-filled message, email response window.
- **Policies (`/policies`)**:
  - Tabbed policy views: 7-Day Exchange & Returns, Shipping & COD, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
